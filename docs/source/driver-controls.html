Driver Controls

MatchAfter START. Nothing moves before START.
Left stick
Drive / strafeRobot-centric: stick forward drives the way the robot faces.
Intake inPull further for more speed. Release to stop.
Launch (hold)Spins up the launcher. Feeds by itself once it's up to speed. Release to stop.
Right stick
TurnLeft and right only.
Intake outReverse, to clear a jam. Both triggers pulled = off.
Reset forwardField-centric only. Point the robot away from you, then press.

Good to know

  • Keep holding for the whole volley. The feeder pauses by itself while the launcher gets back up to speed between shots.
  • The intake runs a little faster while the launcher feeds, to push pieces up out of the hopper.
  • Driving is robot-centric unless the code turns field-centric on. Then resets "forward".
  • Keep hands clear of the launcher flywheel.
